WICareerBuilder v1.0.0

Your Learning · Your Evidence · Your Career.

WICareerBuilder is a local-first career evidence workbench. It organises learning material, preserves projects and troubleshooting history, separates genuine evidence from qualifications, enforces explicit CV approval, supports multiple career-specific CVs, and keeps private/no-web material behind an external-processing boundary.

Original filesNever silently moved, renamed, rewritten or deleted.
Personal dataSQLite stored separately from replaceable application files.
Evidence truthLearning and internal quizzes do not automatically become qualifications.
PrivacyPrivate/no-web material is denied at the lower external-processing boundary.
CV approvalRecommended evidence still requires explicit user approval before CV use.
Product statusV1 engineering release verified; product closure evidence lives with this release.
